## Cube Solver 1.0.0

The first release. It runs in the browser, installs as a PWA that works offline, and installs on Windows as a small desktop app built with Tauri.

### Cubes and input
- 2x2 and 3x3 cubes in 3D. Drag a sticker to turn its layer (mouse, pen or touch), drag the background to rotate the view, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Keyboard turns: U R F D L B, M E S slices, and x y z rotations; Shift gives the prime. Typed algorithms work too.
- Paint any sticker on the 3D cube or on a flat net editor. Impossible states get a specific explanation: colour counts, a mirrored or duplicated piece, a twisted corner, a flipped edge, or a parity swap.
- Reset, random-state scramble, undo/redo, and a move history you can click through.
- Import and export as a facelet string, JSON or a move list. Share links put the cube state in the URL.

### Solving
- **2x2:** always optimal, from a complete table of all 3,674,160 positions.
- **3x3 Fast:** Kociemba's two-phase algorithm. It finds an answer in milliseconds and keeps improving it while it thinks.
- **3x3 Optimal:** IDA* with pattern databases (corners and edges) for the proven-shortest solution in quarter turns. It shows progress live and can be stopped. Short positions are proven even in Fast mode.
- **Ranked next moves:** each move shows its distance to solved, marked as proven or estimated. Ties are broken by a proven lower bound. Beginner mode also weighs how easy each turn is by hand.
- **Method breakdowns:** Layer by layer, CFOP, Roux and ZZ, each split into named steps. Play any step on its own, or all of them.
- Solutions play back with play/pause, step forward and back, and adjustable speed.

### App
- Light and dark themes, a keyboard shortcut sheet (`?`), a first-run intro, a responsive layout for phones, and accessible controls.
- Update check against GitHub releases.
- Solver tables are cached in the browser (IndexedDB), so after the first run the optimal solver starts in about a second instead of rebuilding.
