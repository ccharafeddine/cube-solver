## Cube Solver 1.2.0

This is a big one: every cube size from 2x2 to 7x7, a true-to-life 3D cube you can take apart, four themes, and a new look.

### Bigger cubes
- New 4x4, 5x5, 6x6 and 7x7. Pick a size with the buttons at the top or the keys 2 to 7.
- Big cubes are solved the way people do it by hand: fix parity, solve the centres, pair the edges, then finish like a 3x3. You can play the whole solution or one step at a time.
- Inner layers have their own names, like `2R` for the second layer from the right.
- Dragging a sticker turns exactly the layer you grabbed, in the direction you drag, on every size and from any angle.

### A cube that looks like the real thing
- Pieces are hollow plastic with rounded edges, and the stickers sit slightly inside a black rim.
- The 6x6 and 7x7 have gently rounded (pillowed) faces, like the real V-Cubes. The smaller cubes stay flat, like the real ones.
- **Explode** (or the G key) now works on every size and shows the real mechanism inside: the 2x2's axles, the 3x3's core and screws, the 4x4's grooved ball, and the cross with long stalks inside the 5x5 to 7x7.
- Zoom all the way into the cube to look around inside it.

### Easier to follow
- Scrambles play turn by turn, and every turn goes into the history.
- Click any move in the history or the solution to go there, forwards or backwards, one animated turn at a time.
- The speed slider now controls everything: turns, solutions, method steps and scrambles.
- Next moves are ranked simply by how close each one gets you to solved.
- The tour and help text are rewritten in plain words.

### New look
- Four themes, each with a light and a dark version: Matte (the default), Frutiger Aero, Neon and Neubrutalism.
- A new **Settings** button (the gear) holds the theme, light/dark/system, Check for updates and the version.
- A new logo and app icon, and a bold title font that is built into the app, so it also works offline.
- The layout uses wide screens well, and buttons stay in the same place: long solutions scroll in their own box, and the page no longer jumps sideways.

### Install
- **Windows:** `Cube Solver_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe` installs for the current user without admin rights; the `.msi` is also available. Both are unsigned, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm.
- **Web / PWA:** unzip `cube-solver-web-v1.2.0.zip` onto any static host.
