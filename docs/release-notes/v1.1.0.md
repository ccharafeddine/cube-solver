## Cube Solver 1.1.0

This release adds an interactive tutorial, so anyone can learn cube notation and find their way around the app.

### Notation lessons
- 14 short lessons cover the faces (U D L R F B), prime ('), double turns (2), the slices M E S, wide moves (Rw), the rotations x y z, and quarter-turn counting.
- Each lesson spotlights the layer on the 3D cube, animates the move, then asks you to play it. You can drag the cube, use the keyboard, type the move, or tap an answer button.
- Answers are checked on the cube's state, so `U U` is a valid `U2`. Partial answers get "keep going". Wrong ones get a hint and a fresh cube.
- A 6-question quiz at the end, with an explanation for every answer.

### App tour
- A 12-step spotlight tour covers turning and dragging, the move pad, painting and the net editor, scramble/reset/undo, the solution, Fast vs Optimal, ranked moves, Beginner mode, method breakdowns, history, and share/import/export.

### Everywhere
- Offered on first launch, and skippable. The new 🎓 Tutorial menu resumes or replays the lessons, the quiz or the tour, and shows your progress.
- Progress is saved between sessions.
- Keyboard and screen-reader friendly, respects reduced motion, and docks to the bottom of the screen on phones.
- Your own cube is set aside during the lessons and restored when you close them.

### Install
- **Windows:** `Cube Solver_1.1.0_x64-setup.exe` installs for the current user without admin rights; the `.msi` is also available. Both are unsigned, so SmartScreen may ask you to confirm.
- **Web / PWA:** unzip `cube-solver-web-v1.1.0.zip` onto any static host.
