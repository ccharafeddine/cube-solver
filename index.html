Controls and shortcuts

U R F D L BTurn a face clockwise; add Shift for counter-clockwise
M E SMiddle slices (3x3)
X Y Z or the X Y Z buttonsRotate the whole cube (Shift-click or Shift for prime); no pieces move
Drag a stickerTurn that layer (mouse, pen or touch)
Drag the backgroundRotate the view; scroll or pinch to zoom
Right-drag or Shift+dragRotate the view from anywhere, even over stickers
SpacePlay / pause the solution
→ / ←Step the solution forward / back
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo a move
Alt+S / Alt+RScramble / reset
Alt+VReset the view: white on top, green in front, front-right corner toward you (also undoes x/y/z rotations)
2 / 3Switch to 2x2 / 3x3
1–6 (Paint tab)Pick a paint colour; 0 or Esc stops painting
G or ExplodeExploded view: spread the pieces out, or assemble them again. Turns pause while exploded; painting and orbiting still work
?This help

Move counts use the quarter-turn metric: a half turn (U2) counts as two.
